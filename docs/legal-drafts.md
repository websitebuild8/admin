## Customer terms

### About the service

iGO is operated by {{REGISTERED_LEGAL_NAME}} at {{BUSINESS_ADDRESS}}. The app connects customers with participating restaurants and arranges delivery in the service areas displayed at checkout. The entities responsible for your goods, delivery, and payment are identified in {{APPROVED_CONTRACTING_MODEL}} and on your order details and receipt.

Create an account using accurate information and keep your sign-in credentials secure. You may use only a payment card you are authorized to use. Tell support if you believe your account or a transaction has been used without authorization.

### Placing and paying for an order

Review your items, delivery address, contact information, charges, and total before payment. Prices and the payable currency are displayed before you authorize payment. Applicable taxes and fees are shown at checkout. Delivery times are estimates; we will provide updates when we learn of a material delay.

Orders are submitted only after payment has been verified. Enter your card details through the supported BML payment interface. iGO does not support payment by bank transfer, cash, or an iGO wallet. Keep a copy of your order confirmation, transaction record, and the policies applicable to your purchase.

A payment-processing message is not an order confirmation. If a payment is uncertain, use the order status or support option before paying again. If payment succeeds but confirmation is delayed, we will investigate and either complete submission or resolve the payment appropriately.

### Cancellation and order problems

After payment is verified and the order is confirmed, we do not accept customer cancellation or refunds solely because of a change of mind. You can still report an order or payment problem using Help in the app or {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} / {{SUPPORT_PHONE}}.

This restriction does not remove remedies for non-delivery, incorrect or missing items, unsafe or defective food, duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions, or any other remedy required by applicable law or bank/card rules. Each case will be assessed according to the facts and applicable rights. We do not require you to waive those rights to place an order.

If we or the restaurant cannot fulfil the entire order, we will arrange a refund of the full amount paid for that order, including its delivery and service fees. For a partially affected order, we will assess the appropriate remedy and related charges. We will not substitute an item or increase your payment without your agreement.

Refunds use the original payment route where supported and required by the payment arrangement. We will tell you when a refund is initiated; the time it appears on your account depends on bank processing. We do not promise an unverified fixed posting time. If a refund fails, contact support with the order reference, not your full card details.

### Delivery and food concerns

Provide an accurate entrance pin, building/floor details, and contact information. If delivery cannot be completed, support will investigate the reason and available remedy. We will not apply undisclosed penalties or automatically treat every failed delivery as a forfeited payment.

Restaurant item information should be reviewed before ordering. Contact the restaurant/support about allergies before payment; a delivery note does not guarantee accommodation. This does not remove the restaurant's or operator's food-safety responsibilities. Do not consume food you believe is unsafe; report the issue promptly so we can investigate. Evidence may help, but mandatory rights are not conditioned on an arbitrary short reporting window.

### Complaints and rights

Use Get help with this order or contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} / {{SUPPORT_PHONE}}. We will provide a case reference and progress updates. Our published service target is {{COMPLAINT_RESPONSE_TARGET}}; this target does not shorten your legal rights.

You may also seek assistance from the Maldives Consumer Ombudsman's Office at https://consumer.gov.mv/ or use any other remedy available by law. Contacting iGO does not waive bank dispute rights or require you to abandon a regulator complaint.

These terms are intended for services in the Maldives and are subject to applicable Maldives law, without excluding any mandatory rights that apply. No clause excludes responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded. Changes will be published with a new version; the terms accepted for an existing order remain recorded.

## Privacy notice

This draft describes the intended minimum data practices. Final publication requires a verified data inventory, provider list, hosting regions, and retention schedule. Remove any statements that do not match the built system.

### Who handles information

{{REGISTERED_LEGAL_NAME}} operates iGO. Contact {{PRIVACY_CONTACT}} at {{BUSINESS_ADDRESS}} about personal information. The role of restaurants and other providers is described in {{APPROVED_PRIVACY_RESPONSIBILITIES}}.

### Information and purposes

We use account details and contact information to provide sign-in and support; addresses and order details to fulfil deliveries; payment references, amounts, and statuses to verify payments and resolve billing; and necessary technical/security records to keep the service working and investigate misuse.

Restaurant applicants provide business details and verification documents. Rider applicants provide the identity, licence, vehicle, and payment information required for the approved onboarding process. The exact required documents are listed before collection. These documents are accessible only to staff who need them for verification or administration.

Customers and restaurants can search for a building/address, select a Google Maps suggestion, review the entrance, and add their own building/unit and access instructions. Search text and selected place identifiers are sent through iGO to Google Maps Platform for suggestions and location resolution. Google receives ordinary map/network requests while maps are displayed. Do not include unit numbers, contact details or private delivery instructions in the location-search field. Those details are entered separately for iGO delivery operations.

Google Maps Platform provides the map and location-search services under Google's Terms of Service (https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/) and Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Search suggestions and selected Google labels are transient, and iGO does not retain a copy of Google's landmark catalogue. Google-derived coordinates expire after 26 days and are removed by scheduled database cleanup, including associated order/quote coordinate copies. A saved entrance may then need another lookup; restaurant entrances require review before use. User-entered address text is retained under iGO's published retention schedule. Database backups and recovery copies require a separately confirmed retention/deletion policy before launch.

iGO does not request device GPS permission or collect continuous rider location. Requests are allocated by the rider's selected service area or by an administrator. Only assigned riders receive pickup and delivery entrances; they can see those endpoints on a Google map and open Google Maps for directions. Google Maps handles its own device-location permissions under its own terms. Customers and restaurants receive order milestones and approximate delivery estimates without a live rider map.

iGO does not receive or store full card numbers, CVVs, or bank authentication codes in its own systems. Payment information entered into the supported checkout is processed through BML and its payment providers. We retain only the limited transaction information needed for order and accounting operations.
