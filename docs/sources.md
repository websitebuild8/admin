# Research sources and limitations

Checked 29 September 2026. Sources establish specific requirements, not a certification that the proposed business complies with all laws. Recheck before launch.

1. [Consumer Ombudsman — Ministry](https://trade.gov.mv/en/consumer-ombudsman/) and [official law/regulation downloads](https://consumer.gov.mv/en/downloads/): confirm the consumer-protection framework and complaint route. The full statutory provisions have not been interpreted here. Cancellation exceptions in the drafts are conservative proposed policy, not invented quotations from the Act.
2. [BML public card-acceptance terms](https://www.bankofmaldives.com.mv/storage/file/361/4728/card-acceptance-terms-conditions-en.pdf): gateway section addresses merchant identity/contact details, purchase terms, refund/delivery/privacy/security disclosures and transaction records; general section 5.2 restricts collection for third parties. This is an older accessible public version. A newer indexed document was found at https://www.bankofmaldives.com.mv/storage/file/361/8737/card-acceptance-terms-conditions-en.pdf but could not be fully retrieved in this turn. Obtain current executed terms from BML; do not treat the older PDF as the final applicable agreement.
3. [BML official integration repository](https://github.com/bankofmaldives/bml-connect) links to [current API documentation](https://docs.merchants.bankofmaldives.com.mv/). Detailed API fields and verification algorithms remain unverified; no real BML code was generated.
4. [MIRA tax invoice guidance](https://www.mira.gov.mv/Pages/View/gsttaxinvoice) and [GST regulation](https://www.mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Goods-and-Services-Tax-Regulation-consolidated): reference for invoice and tax review. No tax rate or retention period is assumed for iGO.
5. [MFDA food establishment registration on oneGov](https://one.gov.mv/submissions/66cef3c5-f419-f605-8797-31078ff48e73/new): government registration service references Food Safety Act 6/2024. Confirm establishment and delivery requirements with the competent authority.
6. [Apple account deletion](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app): account creation requires in-app deletion initiation; retention exceptions need explanation.
7. [Google Play account deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en): requires an in-app path and a functional public web request resource for covered apps.
8. [Apple app privacy disclosures](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-privacy): privacy policy URL and accurate data handling declarations.
9. [Android location permissions](https://developer.android.com/develop/sensors-and-location/location/permissions) and [Google background-location policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9799150): permissions/disclosures and review considerations, separate from local legal obligations.

Unresolved: current detailed Maldives privacy/e-commerce obligations; exact consumer-law remedies/deadlines; business licences; employment/contractor treatment; insurance; language requirements; retention periods; BML marketplace and refund arrangement; GST supplier/tax model. These require current authoritative verification and professional review rather than assumptions.
